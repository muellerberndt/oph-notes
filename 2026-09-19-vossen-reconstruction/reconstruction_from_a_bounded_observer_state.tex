{\LARGE\bfseries Reconstruction from a Bounded Observer State}\\[0.7ex]
{\normalsize Compression and recovery in Observer-Patch Holography (OPH)}\\[0.9ex]
{\small Bernhard Mueller \quad$\cdot$\quad Pragma Research Inc.\quad$\cdot$\quad 19 September 2026}

\vspace{-1.5ex}

\begin{abstract}
\noindent In OPH no global computer renders 3D particle coordinates frame by frame.
What we perceive as three-dimensional space, fields and time moving forward are
observer-facing readouts of a computed quotient normal form on a holographic screen.
The substrate has no background clock, no 3D space and no master copy of the world. It
has a finite network of observer patches, a mismatch score on every shared boundary
seam and a local repair move that lowers those scores, and each repair move is a
quantum recovery channel. A bounded observer therefore needs no copy of the world
beyond its boundary. This note makes that quantitative: the relational information
that can be removed from an observer's representation is measured by the conditional
mutual information across its boundary collar, which bounds the best reconstruction
error from above and below.
\end{abstract}

\section{Rendering without a master copy}

Bulk spacetime is read off the repaired normal form. The geometry readout is a
function of that gauge-invariant quotient and does not depend on hidden carrier
coordinates, gauge representatives or the repair schedule \cit{SM, Thm.~6.56}. On its
declared branch the spacetime paper recovers flat $1{+}3$ causal order, Lorentz-frame
kinematics and, under further stated premises, the Einstein equation up to a
cosmological constant \cit{ST}. There is no master database holding the state of
every atom. The computation can be split over any number of workers and shards, and
every admissible execution gives the same observer readouts whatever the partition,
schedule or restart history \cit{C, Thm.~9.5, Cor.~9.6}. An observer holds a finite
patch whose records form a commutative central algebra, and its local world is
evaluated when it reads that algebra \cit{C, Thm.~7.2}.

Stefaan Vossen asked, in a discussion of compression and what he calls holographic
beingness, how much relational information can be removed from a representation
before an independent operator can no longer reconstruct the relevant larger state.
OPH answers this with the collar Markov condition and its Petz/Fawzi--Renner recovery
channels (Section~3). The repair move of the substrate is itself such a recovery
channel, so the one update rule of an OPH world is reconstruction from a compressed
boundary. Vossen's slogan ``understanding is reconstruction from compressed
representation'' is, in OPH, the dynamical law.

\section{The substrate}

\textbf{Patch net} \cit{C, Def.~2.1--2.2, Prop.~2.3}. A finite connected graph
$G=(V,E)$; patch $i$ has a finite state space $S_i$, the global space is
$\Sigma=\prod_i S_i$, and each overlap $e=\{i,j\}$ has an interface alphabet $I_e$
with restriction maps $\pi_{i,e}:S_i\to I_e$. The mismatch potential
\[
\Phi(s)=\sum_{e=\{i,j\}\in E} w_e\,d_e\bigl(\pi_{i,e}(s_i),\pi_{j,e}(s_j)\bigr),
\qquad w_e>0,\quad d_e(a,b)=0\iff a=b,
\]
vanishes exactly on the globally consistent set $C=\Phi^{-1}(0)$, a finite constraint code.

\textbf{Repair} \cit{C, Def.~3.1--3.2}. Patch $i$ carries a collar chart
$A_i\!-\!B_i\!-\!D_i$ around its overlaps. A repair move $T_i$ changes only patch $i$
and is induced by a recovery move on that collar: the exact Markov splice when
$I(A_i{:}D_i\,|\,B_i)=0$, otherwise
$(\id_{A_i}\otimes\mathcal R_i)(\omega_{A_iB_i})$ with $\mathcal R_i$ a
Petz/Fawzi--Renner recovery channel. A move is accepted only if it strictly lowers
the score $\Phi_i$ of the overlaps it touches.

\textbf{Normal form} \cit{C, Thm.~3.25, Cor.~3.26, Thm.~5.2}. Termination, local
confluence on the gauge quotient $Q=\Sigma/\Gamma$, repair completeness and Newman's
lemma give each
initial state $s$ a unique quotient normal form $\overline{\nf}(q(s))\in q(C)$,
reached by every maximal asynchronous repair run. Every quotient observable takes the
same value for every update order, and gauge-equivalent inputs reach the same normal
form. Bare consensus fixes no metric by itself \cit{C, Thm.~2.6}; geometry enters
through the declared readout branch of \cit{SM, ST}.

\textbf{Pairwise agreement is not enough} \cit{C, Thm.~4.1, Cor.~4.2}. For labels in
an abelian group, the seam equations $x_v-x_u=b_e$ have a global solution if and only
if $\sum_{e\in\gamma}\varepsilon_\gamma(e)\,b_e=0$ on every cycle $\gamma$. On a
$\mathbb Z_2$ triangle with $b=(0,0,1)$ each seam is satisfiable on its own and the
triangle is not. In the language of tomography, one projection never suffices, and
global consistency is a condition on cycles of views.

\textbf{Records} \cit{C, Def.~7.1, Thm.~7.2}. On a completed slice with accessible
algebra $\mathcal A_t$, the record projectors $\widehat P_a(t)\in Z(\mathcal A_t)$
generate a finite commutative algebra $\mathcal Z_{\mathrm{rec}}(t)$. For an event
$E$ in it, $\mathbb P_t(E)=\Tr(\rho_t\widehat P_E)$, the declared L\"uders update is
$\rho_t|_E=\widehat P_E\rho_t\widehat P_E/\Tr(\rho_t\widehat P_E)$, and an immediate
reread returns $E$ with probability one.

\section{Removal and reconstruction}

Let $A$ be the patch interior, $B$ its collar and $D$ everything beyond. The observer
holds $\rho_{AB}$; the larger state is $\rho_{ABD}$. With von Neumann entropies $S$
in nats and modular Hamiltonians $K_X=-\ln\rho_X$,
\[
I(A{:}D\,|\,B)=S(AB)+S(BD)-S(B)-S(ABD)
=\Tr\rho_{ABD}\bigl(K_{AB}+K_{BD}-K_B-K_{ABD}\bigr)\;\ge\;0
\]
\cit{LR; C, App.~A}. It measures the correlation between $A$ and $D$ that $B$ does
not carry. Write
\[
\delta_\star:=\min_{\mathcal R:\,B\to BD}\tfrac12\bigl\|\rho_{ABD}-(\id_A\otimes\mathcal R)(\rho_{AB})\bigr\|_1
\]
for the best reconstruction error of an operator that acts on the collar alone.

\textbf{Exact case.} $I(A{:}D\,|\,B)=0$ if and only if
$\rho_{ABD}=(\id_A\otimes\mathcal P)(\rho_{AB})$ for the Petz map \cit{P1, P2}
\[
\mathcal P_{B\to BD}(X)=\rho_{BD}^{1/2}\bigl(\rho_B^{-1/2}X\rho_B^{-1/2}\otimes 1_D\bigr)\rho_{BD}^{1/2}
\qquad(\text{inverses on supports}),
\]
and if and only if
$\mathcal H_B=\bigoplus_\alpha\mathcal H_{b_L^\alpha}\otimes\mathcal H_{b_R^\alpha}$ with
\cit{HJPW}
\[
\rho_{ABD}=\bigoplus_\alpha p_\alpha\,\rho^{(\alpha)}_{Ab_L^\alpha}\otimes\rho^{(\alpha)}_{b_R^\alpha D}.
\]
On this form the splice identity of \cit{C, Thm.~A.1} holds: replacing each
$\rho^{(\alpha)}_{b_R^\alpha D}$ by any state $\sigma^{(\alpha)}_{b_R^\alpha D'}$ on
the same sectors leaves $\Tr(X\rho)$ unchanged for every observable $X$ on
$A\cup b_L$. The entire exterior can be removed and exchanged; what must be kept is
the sector structure of the collar.

\textbf{Approximate case.} For every state, with $d_A=\dim\mathcal H_A$ and $h$ the
binary entropy in nats,
\begin{align}
\delta_\star^2&\le 1-e^{-I(A:D|B)}\le I(A{:}D\,|\,B),\label{eq:fr}\\
I(A{:}D\,|\,B)&\le 2\delta_\star\ln d_A+(1+\delta_\star)\,h\!\bigl(\tfrac{\delta_\star}{1+\delta_\star}\bigr).\label{eq:w}
\end{align}
Bound \eqref{eq:fr} is the Fawzi--Renner theorem
$I(A{:}D\,|\,B)\ge-2\ln F\bigl(\rho_{ABD},(\id_A\otimes\mathcal R)(\rho_{AB})\bigr)$,
$F(\rho,\sigma)=\|\sqrt\rho\sqrt\sigma\|_1$ \cit{FR}, combined with
$\tfrac12\|\rho-\sigma\|_1\le\sqrt{1-F^2}$ \cit{FvdG}; the channel $\mathcal R$ can
be taken universal, built from $\rho_{BD}$ alone \cit{JRSWW, Cor.~4.1}. For
\eqref{eq:w}, let $\sigma=(\id_A\otimes\mathcal R)(\rho_{AB})$ attain $\delta_\star$.
Data processing gives $I(A{:}BD)_\sigma\le I(A{:}B)_\rho$ and $\sigma_A=\rho_A$, so
$I(A{:}D\,|\,B)_\rho=I(A{:}BD)_\rho-I(A{:}B)_\rho\le S(A|BD)_\sigma-S(A|BD)_\rho$,
which Winter's continuity bound controls \cit{W, Lemma~2}. Bounded observables
inherit \eqref{eq:fr}: $|\Tr X(\rho-\sigma)|\le 2\|X\|_\infty\delta_\star$.

\textbf{Removal criterion.} Let the observer keep only a part $B'$ of its boundary
representation, $B=B'B''$. Rebuilding the dropped part together with the exterior is
the same problem with $B\to B'$ and $D\to B''D$. For a target error $\eta$, an
operator acting on $B'$ alone succeeds whenever
\[
I(A{:}B''D\,|\,B')\le-\ln(1-\eta^2),
\]
and by \eqref{eq:w} no operator on $B'$ succeeds once
$I(A{:}B''D\,|\,B')>2\eta\ln d_A+(1+\eta)\,h\bigl(\eta/(1+\eta)\bigr)$. The relational
information that can be removed is the part whose removal keeps this conditional
mutual information below tolerance. The decoding rule is fixed by
$\rho_{B'B''D}$, the statistics of the world beyond the interior, so the observer
needs no copy of $D$, only access to the decoding rule.

\textbf{Which boundary factor faces the observer.} Zero conditional mutual
information fixes the split of $B$ from the state. The geometric statements of OPH
need that split to agree with the preselected edge split of the collar, a separate
condition called Markov-split alignment \cit{ST, Sec.~2.3}. Four qubits with
$\rho=\Phi_{AB_R}\otimes\Phi_{B_LD}$ (Bell pairs across the cut) have
$I(A{:}D\,|\,B)=0$ and $I(A{:}B_R)=2\ln2$: the compression is exact, and the factor
of $B$ that faces the observer is $B_R$. Storage and representation separate
already at the level of a single collar.

\textbf{What the part carries about the whole.} For a gauge-invariant collar with
sectors $\alpha$ of dimension $d_\alpha$, the one-sided state obeys \cit{FL, Thm.~5.2}
\[
S(\rho_L)=H(p)+\sum_\alpha p_\alpha S(\rho_{L,\alpha})+\sum_\alpha p_\alpha\ln d_\alpha ,
\]
and the last term is the expectation of the central observable
$Z_L=\sum_\alpha(\ln d_\alpha)P^L_\alpha$. The boundary imprint sits in the
commutative center, the same place as the records.

\section{Compression vocabulary in OPH}

\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.29\linewidth}p{0.67\linewidth}@{}}
larger state & $\rho_{ABD}$\\
storage & $\rho_{AB}$: the patch and its collar\\
representation & collar sectors $\alpha$ and records in the commutative center\\
decoding rules & recovery channel $\mathcal R_{B\to BD}$, fixed by $\rho_{BD}$\\
reconstruction & $(\id_A\otimes\mathcal R)(\rho_{AB})$, error bounded by \eqref{eq:fr}--\eqref{eq:w}\\
what can be removed & all of $D$, and any $B''$ with $I(A{:}B''D\,|\,B')\approx0$\\
additional perspectives & overlapping patches; consistency is a cycle condition, and
repair gives all of them one normal form
\end{tabular}

In OPH the relation between a part and the whole is fixed by recoverability, and the
whole that observers share is the quotient normal form, which no single patch stores.
Vossen's weaker proposition, that a bounded being carries recoverable relations to a
whole it never becomes, holds there with the conditional mutual information as its
measure.
