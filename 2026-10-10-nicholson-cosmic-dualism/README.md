# Reconsidering Cosmic Dualism: audit and corrected edition

John Glen Nicholson's paper "Reconsidering Cosmic Dualism: A Two-Realm Framework" (Information
Physics Institute, draft dated 2026-10-10), audited and rebuilt on 2026-10-10.

Files:

- `Reconsidering-Cosmic-Dualism-fable.pdf` and `.md`: the corrected paper (16 sections, 147
  references). The Markdown links the images in `figures/`.
- `Reconsidering-Cosmic-Dualism-fable-audit.pdf` and `.md`: the audit report with every correction,
  the citation repairs, the additions, the figure-permission items and the decisions left to the
  author.
- `figures/`: the 25 figures extracted from the original PDF. Four are third-party images (credits
  in the captions); their licences are listed in the audit as open items.

The PDF was rendered from the Markdown with WeasyPrint. Direct downloads are attached to the
release `nicholson-2026-10-10`.
